# AskList

What someone needs from you on this task, each ask with the words it came from.

A `ul.asks` of `li.ask`: the asker in 600 weight, the ask's summary in `body`, and a `source` link (`ext`, `me-text`) to the message it came from; under it each quote as a `blockquote.quote`, in `muted`, with a `rule` bar of `strip-row` width on its leading edge.

## Consumer provides
The task's asks: asker, summary, permalink and quotes, as the extraction or the plugin recorded them.

## Do / don't
- Do quote the source's own words, at most a few lines each, so you can judge the ask without opening the thread.
- Don't style a quote as a message of the timeline: a quote is evidence, not a turn of the conversation.
