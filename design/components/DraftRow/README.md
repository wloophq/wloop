# DraftRow

*Proposed.* One Outbox draft: kind, recipient, two lines of text, policy, Approve.

The `sg-draft` glyph, a meta line with the draft kind as a flag and the recipient as a SourceChip, the body clamped to two lines, and a policy line naming what proposed it (an intent or a proposal rule, both from the profile). Approve is `primary`; the full text and Discard live in the inspector.
