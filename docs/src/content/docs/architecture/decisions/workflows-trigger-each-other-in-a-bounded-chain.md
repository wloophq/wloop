---
title: Workflows trigger each other in a bounded chain
description: Every verb records its event with its actor and its chain, and an event trigger stops at its depth and never re-enters its chain.
---

- **Context**: a workflow reacts to a verb through its `verb_<verb>` event. A path that runs a verb and records nothing hides it from the timeline and from the profile; a workflow reacting to another's move (clarify, then set the kind, then start a turn) needs that move recorded, and an unbounded chain of workflows could loop.
- **Decision**: a verb that changes a stored task records its event on the task's unit in the transaction that changes it, or, when another service runs it (a message's, the bridge to a unit's action, reply), once that succeeded; a verb that changes nothing records nothing, and neither does one whose handler is `seen`. The event's actor is `<actor>:<source>` (`person:cockpit`, `workflow:triage`), and its data carries the chain of workflows that led to it. A workflow's event trigger fires only for the actors in `trigger.actors`, only on an event whose chain is shorter than `trigger.depth` (3 by default, at most 5), and never on an event whose chain it is in already.
- **Consequence**: a new path that runs a verb goes through `Service.Do` or `RecordVerb` with its actor, and a workflow's `task.verb` carries the chain of the event that planned it; a reaction to a verb served elsewhere is lost if the daemon stops between the verb and its record.
