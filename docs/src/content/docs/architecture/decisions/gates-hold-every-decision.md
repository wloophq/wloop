---
title: Gates hold every decision
description: A decision the loop could wait on is a gate; you decide which wait for you, no rule that releases one reads an untrusted fact, and no unattended agent resolves one.
---

- **Context**: a send, a run or an answer decided by a rule or by an agent would let a thread's or an agent's text act under your name: an agent writes task properties, a classification's output and a brief's extra fields, and a thread's author writes what the plugin reads. And a loop that asks before everything makes more work than it saves.
- **Decision**: every decision the loop could wait on you for (a draft, a run waiting for approval or trust, a question, an agent's post, a proposal, a gated step) is a gate, and drafts, plan items, messages and proposals keep only their own lifecycles. You decide, per gate kind, name, workflow, intent or plugin, whether a gate waits for you, waits for its window or is released at once, in a `[gates.approval]` table; the bundled profile only sets defaults. Every gate opens under the approval of what opened it: a workflow's gates, its runs', its drafts', its `gate.open` steps' and its verbs', under the workflow's, an intent's drafts under the intent's, and the rest under `gates.toml`'s. A `[gates.approval]` rule that releases a gate (a draft gate's rule of any mode, any other gate's of mode `send`, `run` or `delay`) fails to load when it reads a fact the facts table marks untrusted, a brief's extra field, a classification's output or a property an agent or a plugin writes, in an intent's or a workflow's manifest and in `config.toml`; what selects the work, a workflow's `when`, may read any. A proposal that pairs two tasks you took apart waits for you whatever its approval says. A resolution by a plugin is refused, and so is one by an agent unless it comes through a session you drive, as [Authority comes from you](/architecture/decisions/authority-comes-from-you/) says.
- **Consequence**: what an agent writes can inform you, order your lists and pick which workflow asks, never release a post or a run by itself; a new fact, property or record kind that an agent or a plugin writes is untrusted from its first use, and a rule that needs it waits for you. Where the loop acts at once is where your approval says so, and what it did is named on the timeline.
- **Enforced by**: `TestApprovalRulesReadTrustedFacts`, `TestNoUnattendedAgentResolvesAGate`, `TestAGatedVerbOpensItsGateThroughTheService`, `TestASlackItemLinkingAPullRequestWithATaskIsMergedIntoIt`, `TestAFoldThatWaitsForYouMergesOnceApproved`, `TestASplitFoldWaitsForYouEverAfter`, `TestAPairYouUnrelatedIsNeverRelatedAgainOnItsOwn`. See [Gates](/concepts/approval/#gates).
